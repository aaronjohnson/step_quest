Privacy Policy

Step Quest

Last updated: January 24, 2026

Step Quest is designed with privacy as a core principle. We collect minimal data, store everything locally on your device, and never share your information with third parties.

Data We Access

Step Quest accesses the following data solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Step Count Data: We read your daily step count from your device's pedometer or Health Connect to track your walking progress and calculate XP.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Location or GPS data
  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Health data beyond step counts
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or tracking data

Data Storage

All your game data (level, XP, achievements, streaks) is stored locally on your device using secure local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any servers.

Third-Party Services

Step Quest does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If you use the optional tip jar feature, payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store any payment information.

Health Connect Integration

When you grant Health Connect permissions, Step Quest reads only your step count data. We do not access any other health metrics. You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device's Health Connect settings.

Children's Privacy

Step Quest does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for users of all ages.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all Step Quest data by:

  • Using the "Reset Game Data" option in the app's settings
  • Clearing the app's data through your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
