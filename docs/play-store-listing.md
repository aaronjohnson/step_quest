## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Step Quest transforms your daily walking into an exciting RPG adventure. Every step you take earns XP, helping you level up from a humble Walker to a legendary Dragon Lord.

**Features:**
• Real-time step tracking with pedometer
• Health Connect integration for accurate step data
• Level progression system with XP rewards
• Daily streak tracking to build healthy habits
• Achievement system with unlockable badges
• Beautiful, game-inspired UI
• Wear OS support for your smartwatch
• No ads — ever

**Your Journey Awaits:**
Start as a Walker and evolve through the ranks:
Walker → Runner → Warrior → Knight → Champion → Monarch → Dragon Lord

**Privacy First:**
Step Quest only accesses step count data. No location tracking, no personal data collection, no ads. Your walking journey stays private.

**Support Development:**
Love Step Quest? Optional tip jar lets you support ongoing development. No features are locked — tips are purely voluntary.

Available in English, German, Spanish, Hindi, Japanese, and Portuguese.
